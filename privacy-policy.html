Privacy Policy — Volum

1. Introduction

Volum (“we,” “our,” or “the app”) is a fitness and workout tracking application for Android and iOS. This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled when you use Volum, as of the date above.

We aim to describe our practices accurately. If the app’s behavior or our use of third-party services changes, we will update this policy and the “Last updated” date. By using Volum, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

Important: This policy is a plain-language summary for users and stores. It is not personalized legal advice. Laws differ by country and region; if you need advice for your situation, consult a qualified attorney.

2. Your workout data stays on your device

The core data that describes your training — workouts, exercises, sets, reps, weights, rest times, session history, achievements, preferences, and optional profile fields (such as name, age, sex, height, or weight used for recommendations) — is stored locally on your device using on-device storage (for example app storage and the device file system). Sensitive profile fields may be stored in encrypted form on the device.

We do not operate a Volum-owned cloud service that stores your full workout logs, exercise photos, or profile biometrics for our own use. AI Coach and tracking features in the app work from data on your device as implemented in the version you install.

Data leaves your device when you choose to export or share it (for example a backup file), when you send an optional bug report, or in the limited third-party cases described in Section 3 (for example purchases, ads on the free tier, or crash diagnostics).

3. Third-party services

We use the following categories of services. Each has its own privacy policy; we encourage you to read them. Their practices govern data they process.

4. Advertising and consent

If you use Volum on the free tier, you may see ads served through Google AdMob. Premium subscribers are not shown these ads.

Where required by law, the app may show a consent flow (UMP) before personalized ads are used. Depending on your region and choices, ads may be non-personalized or limited. On iOS, our first public releases prioritize simple, policy-compliant setups (for example without an extra App Tracking Transparency prompt solely for ads) while still following Google and Apple rules.

5. Permissions

  • Camera — To take photos of exercises. Photos are stored in the app’s storage on your device unless you export them yourself.
  • Storage / media (Android) — To save and access photos and exported files, as required by the OS.
  • Notifications — If you enable reminders, to schedule local notifications.

You can revoke permissions in system settings; some features may stop working.

6. Security

Workout and profile data is stored on your device. A random on-device identifier may be used for local encryption key derivation; it is not sent to our servers for tracking.

No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure. You are responsible for device security (passcode, OS updates) and for any copies you export.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • On your device: Data remains until you delete it in the app, use clear-data options where available, or uninstall Volum.
  • Bug reports: Retained by Formspree and our inbox only as long as needed to respond.
  • Purchases: Apple and Google (and RevenueCat as processor) retain purchase records according to their policies for billing, tax, and fraud prevention.
  • Ad / crash partners: Retention per Google’s and Sentry’s policies.

To ask about data held by us through bug reports or similar voluntary submissions, email us. We cannot delete Apple’s, Google’s, Google’s ad systems’, or RevenueCat’s records on your behalf; use platform and partner tools where available.

8. Children

Volum is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

9. International users and transfers

Volum may be used in many countries. We are based in the United States. Third parties listed in Section 3 may process data in the United States or other regions. By using features that rely on those services, you understand that cross-border transfers may occur as permitted by applicable law.

10. Regional privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing, to port data, or to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. You may also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority.

  • EEA, UK, and similar regimes: We rely on bases such as contract (providing the app and purchases), legitimate interests (for example security and crash diagnostics, where applicable), and consent where required (for example certain advertising choices). Contact us to exercise rights; you may also contact your local data protection authority.

We may need to verify your request where the law allows. We will not discriminate against you for exercising lawful privacy rights.
