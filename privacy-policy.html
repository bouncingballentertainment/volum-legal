2. Your workout data stays on your device

The core data that describes your training — workouts, exercises, sets, reps, weights, rest times, session history, achievements, preferences, and optional profile fields (such as name, age, sex, height, or weight used for recommendations) — is stored locally on your device using on-device storage (for example app storage and the device file system). Sensitive profile fields may be stored in encrypted form on the device.

We do not operate a Volum-owned cloud service that stores your full workout logs, exercise photos, or profile biometrics for our own use. AI Coach and tracking features in the app work from data on your device as implemented in the version you install.

To give you a 14-day Premium trial, the App stores your first-launch date on your device. This is not sent to us.

Data leaves your device when you choose to export or share it (for example a backup file), when you send an optional bug report, when you sign up on the Volum website, or in the limited third-party cases described in Section 3 (for example purchases or ads on the free tier). Volum ships no crash-reporting or error-reporting SDK — nothing about app errors is collected or sent automatically. When you use Settings → About → Report a Bug, you can optionally check a box to include a short technical summary of recent in-app errors (timestamps and error messages, no workout data) to help us debug; this is only ever sent if you choose to include it.

If you use Volum on the free tier, you may see ads served through Google AdMob. Premium subscribers are not shown these ads.

In the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, Google's consent message asks for your choice before ads are served to you. If you decline, you may still see ads, but they are limited and not personalized. 7. Data retention and deletion

  • On your device: Data remains until you delete it in the app, use clear-data options where available, or uninstall Volum.
    • "Delete Workout" (main menu): deletes your current workouts, but their structure (names, exercises, and order — not photos, session history, or personal records) is retained on-device for the last 10 such deletions, so it can be re-imported later. Re-import of this saved structure requires a Premium subscription.
    • "Clear Data" (Settings > Data and Storage): permanently and immediately deletes all workout data, including this recovery history — it is not recoverable.
  • Bug reports and support emails: Kept for up to 12 months after the issue is resolved, unless we need to keep them longer for legal reasons.
  • Website waitlist emails: Kept until you unsubscribe or the launch notification is sent, then deleted within 6 months.
  • Purchases: Apple and Google (and RevenueCat as processor) retain purchase records according to their policies for billing, tax, and fraud prevention.
  • Ad partners: Retention per Google's and Meta's policies.

To ask about data held by us through bug reports, waitlist signups, or similar voluntary submissions, email us. We cannot delete Apple's, Google's, Google's ad systems', or RevenueCat's records on your behalf; use platform and partner tools where available.
