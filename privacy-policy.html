Privacy Policy — Volum

Last updated: May 6, 2026

App: Volum
Developer: Bouncing Ball Entertainment
Contact: bouncingballentertainment@gmail.com

1. Introduction

Volum (“we,” “our,” or “the app”) is a fitness and workout tracking application for Android and iOS. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Volum.

By using Volum, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

2. Your workout data stays on your device

The core data that describes your training — workouts, exercises, sets, reps, weights, rest times, session history, achievements, preferences, and optional profile fields (such as name, age, sex, height, or weight used for recommendations) — is stored locally on your device using on-device storage (for example app storage and the device file system). Sensitive profile fields may be stored in encrypted form on the device.

We do not upload your workout logs, exercise photos, or profile biometrics to our servers. There is no Volum-owned cloud database of your training content.

Data leaves your device only when you choose to export or share it (for example a backup file), or in the limited third-party cases described in Section 3.

3. Third-party services

We use the following categories of services. Each has its own privacy policy; we encourage you to read them.

Service Purpose Typical data involved
Apple App Store / Google Play In-app purchases and subscriptions (including free trials where offered) Payment is processed by the platform. We do not receive your full card number. The platform shares purchase-related signals with our subscription partner (RevenueCat) so the app can unlock Premium features.
RevenueCat Subscription status, restore purchases, entitlements Purchase and entitlement information from Apple/Google, app and device identifiers as described in RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy. Not your workout database.
Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) Advertising on the free tier only (Premium is ad-free) Ad delivery may use identifiers and technical data under Google’s Privacy Policy. We aim to use non-personalized or limited personalization flows where appropriate; see Section 4.
Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) Consent for ads where required (for example EEA / UK) Consent choices and related signals as described by Google.
Formspree Optional “Report a Bug” emails Only the text you submit in the bug form and related metadata Formspree needs to deliver the message. See Formspree’s Privacy Policy.
Sentry (if enabled in your build) Crash and error diagnostics Technical diagnostics (for example stack traces, app version, device/OS). We do not use Sentry to collect your workout content. Enabled only when our error-reporting configuration is active.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use a separate third-party analytics product to track how you tap through the app for marketing purposes.

4. Advertising and consent

If you use Volum on the free tier, you may see ads served through Google AdMob. Premium subscribers are not shown these ads.

Where required by law, the app may show a consent flow (UMP) before personalized ads are used. Depending on your region and choices, ads may be non-personalized or limited. On iOS, our first public releases prioritize simple, policy-compliant setups (for example without an extra App Tracking Transparency prompt solely for ads) while still following Google and Apple rules.

5. Permissions

  • Camera — To take photos of exercises. Photos are stored in the app’s storage on your device unless you export them yourself.
  • Storage / media (Android) — To save and access photos and exported files, as required by the OS.
  • Notifications — If you enable reminders, to schedule local notifications.

You can revoke permissions in system settings; some features may stop working.

6. Security

Workout and profile data is stored on your device. A random on-device identifier may be used for local encryption key derivation; it is not sent to our servers for tracking.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • On your device: Data remains until you delete it in the app, use clear-data options where available, or uninstall Volum.
  • Bug reports: Retained by Formspree and our inbox only as long as needed to respond.
  • Purchases: Apple and Google (and RevenueCat as processor) retain purchase records according to their policies for billing, tax, and fraud prevention.
  • Ad / crash partners: Retention per Google’s and Sentry’s policies.

8. Children

Volum is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe this has occurred.

9. International users

Volum may be used in many countries. Third parties listed above may process data in the United States or other regions. By using the app and those features, you understand that such processing may occur.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
