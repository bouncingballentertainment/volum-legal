Volum Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Overview

Volum is a fitness tracking app. Your data stays on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal information.

Data We Process

All data is stored locally on your device:

  • Workout data – exercises, sets, reps, weight, rest times
  • Profile – optional name, age, sex for AI Coach recommendations
  • Photos – exercise photos you take with the camera (stored on device only)

Permissions We Use

  • Camera – To take photos of exercises. Photos are saved only on your device.
  • Storage (Read/Write) – To save and load photos and app data on your device.

We do not upload photos or any data to external servers.

Data Sharing

We do not share your data with third parties. All data remains on your device.

Data Deletion

You can delete all app data via Settings. Uninstalling the app removes all stored data from your device.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us via the Report a Bug option in the app (Settings → About → Report a Bug).
