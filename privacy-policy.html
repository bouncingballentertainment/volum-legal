Last updated: May 6, 2026

1. Introduction

Volum is a fitness tracking app designed to help you log workouts, track progress, and reach your training goals. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights. By using Volum, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Workout Data Storage

Your core workout data (exercises, sets, reps, weights, rest times, and progress) is stored locally on your device. Volum does not operate its own cloud server to store your workout data. We do not sell your personal information for money.

4. Advertising and Consent

The free tier of Volum may display ads served by Google AdMob. Where required by law (such as in the EEA/UK), the app shows a consent flow before serving personalized ads. Premium subscribers are not shown in-app ads controlled by us.

5. Permissions

  • Camera / Storage / Media: Used only when you choose to attach a photo to a log entry. We do not access your camera or photos without your action.
  • Notifications: Used for rest-timer alerts and optional reminders. You can disable these in device settings.

6. Security

Your workout data is stored on your device using the platform's local storage mechanisms. You are responsible for your device's security (screen lock, encryption settings). We do not transmit your workout data to our servers.

7. Data Retention

On-device data is retained until you delete it or uninstall the app. Bug reports submitted via Formspree are retained temporarily for investigation and then deleted. Crash reports via Sentry are retained per Sentry's retention policy.

8. Children

Volum is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age minimum in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us so we can delete it.

9. International Users

Bouncing Ball Entertainment is based in the United States. If you use Volum from outside the U.S., your data (such as bug reports or crash logs) may be transferred to and processed in the U.S. or other countries where our third-party services operate.

10. Regional Privacy Rights

EEA / UK (GDPR / UK GDPR): You have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict, or port your personal data, and to object to certain processing. Contact us to exercise these rights.

California (CPRA): You have the right to know, delete, correct, and opt-out of sale/sharing of personal information. We do not sell personal information for money. Contact us for requests.

Other jurisdictions: We aim to respect applicable privacy laws worldwide. Contact us with any privacy requests.

11. Fitness Disclaimer

Volum is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Any suggestions from the app (including AI Coach recommendations) are for informational purposes only. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting or changing an exercise program.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy where permitted by law.

13. Contact

Email: bouncingballentertainment@gmail.com
You may also submit privacy requests via the in-app bug report feature.

O Volum não é destinado a crianças menores de 13 anos (ou a idade mínima aplicável no seu país). Não coletamos intencionalmente informações pessoais de crianças. Se acredita que uma criança nos forneceu dados pessoais, entre em contato para que possamos excluí-los.

EEE / Reino Unido (RGPD / UK GDPR): Você tem direitos de acesso, retificação, apagamento, restrição, portabilidade dos seus dados pessoais e de se opor a determinados tratamentos. Entre em contato para exercer esses direitos.

O Volum não é um dispositivo médico e não fornece aconselhamento médico. Quaisquer sugestões do aplicativo (incluindo recomendações do AI Coach) são apenas para fins informativos. Consulte sempre um profissional de saúde qualificado antes de iniciar ou alterar um programa de exercícios.
